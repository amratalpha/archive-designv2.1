How to interpret the report numbers

Period flows vs snapshots

Received and completed/archived count events within the selected range. Pending, overdue and total backlog count unfinished records at the end of that range. Current workflow and lifecycle panels are explicitly current snapshots; the prototype does not reconstruct historical retention or workflow transitions.

Comparison periods

Daily means the demo date; weekly starts Monday; month, quarter, half-year and year start at their calendar boundary and run to the demo date. Custom uses inclusive dates. Comparisons use the immediately preceding range with the same number of days, so partial periods remain comparable. Completed-period comparison can be requested with custom dates.

SLA and backlog definitions

Backlog is any received record not yet completed at period end. Overdue backlog is the subset whose due timestamp has passed. Pending tasks are the subset requiring the officer’s explicit workflow action. Completed-within-SLA is the percentage of completed records whose completion timestamp is no later than their modeled due timestamp.

Data, persistence and controls

632 synthetic records are fixed to 28 October 2026 for repeatable review. The initial dashboard has 7 pending actions, 3 capture batches, 412 archived in the last 30 days, and 58 retention gates split into 51 reviews and 7 transfers. Local edits are demonstrative, stored in the browser where supported, and do not update any live repository. File-browser storage behavior can vary.

Suggested review sequence

  1. Open Needs attention → Overdue, inspect the SLA and required action, and open a task.
  2. Return to the dashboard and click each of the four KPI cards. Confirm that each record-list total matches its card.
  3. Open Classification & record quality, inspect the proposal, correct a record series or metadata and confirm. Return to the dashboard to see the quality total update.
  4. Inspect the four lifecycle queues. Open a legal hold to see why progression is blocked; resolve a retention exception before completing its review.
  5. Open management reporting. Change periods and departments, compare the trends, drill into a department volume or user’s completed count, and export the selected data.
